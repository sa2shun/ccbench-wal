\section{Proposal: Ayame}
\label{sec:design}

A frontier $F$ is a vector of per-shard log positions.  $F \fjoin F'$
denotes the element-wise maximum, $F \le F'$ holds iff $F[i] \le F'[i]$
for every shard $i$, and $\bot$ denotes the empty frontier whose
positions are all zero.  $D$ denotes the durable vector, where $D[i]$ is
the largest log position of shard $i$ that is known to be durable;
each $D[i]$ advances monotonically.

Ayame consists of three worker-side procedures that collect
dependencies during execution (Algorithm~\ref{alg:ayame-dep}), a
worker-side commit protocol that appends the log record and publishes
the closed frontier (Algorithm~\ref{alg:ayame-commit}), and a
committer-side protocol that returns durable acknowledgments
(Algorithm~\ref{alg:ayame-ack}).

\begin{algorithmblock}[t]
\algcaption{Worker-side dependency collection.}
\label{alg:ayame-dep}
\algline{\textbf{procedure} \textsc{Begin}$(T)$}
\algline{\algind $T.\dep \gets \bot$}
\algskip
\algline{\textbf{procedure} \textsc{ReadVersion}$(T, v)$}
\algline{\algind add $v$ to $T$'s read set}
\algline{\algind \textbf{if} $T$ is known read-only \textbf{then return}
         \algcom{SI read-only fast path}}
\algline{\algind $T.\dep \gets T.\dep \fjoin v.\wfr$
         \algcom{the writer of $v$}
         \label{ln:dep-read}}
\algskip
\algline{\textbf{procedure} \textsc{OverwriteVersion}$(T, v)$}
\algline{\algind add the new version to $T$'s write set}
\algline{\algind $T.\dep \gets T.\dep \fjoin v.\wfr \fjoin v.\rfr$
         \algcom{the writer of $v$ and its readers}
         \label{ln:dep-write}}
\end{algorithmblock}

\begin{algorithmblock}[t]
\algcaption{Worker-side commit protocol.}
\label{alg:ayame-commit}
\algline{\textbf{Input:} transaction $T$ with dependency frontier $T.\dep$}
\algline{$T.c \gets$ next commit timestamp of ERMIA/SSN
         \algcom{logical LSN; no WAL-side counter}
         \label{ln:cstamp}}
\algline{\textbf{if} \textsc{SsnValidate}$(T)$ fails
         \textbf{then} abort $T$ \textbf{and return}
         \algcom{exclusion check with $T.c$}}
\algline{\textbf{if} $T$'s write set is empty \textbf{then}
         \algcom{read-only}}
\algline{\algind \textsc{Register}$(T.\dep, T)$ \textbf{and return}
         \algcom{no log record; Algorithm~\ref{alg:ayame-ack}}
         \label{ln:ro-register}}
\algline{$s \gets$ WAL shard owned by this worker}
\algline{$r \gets$ \textsc{BuildRecord}$(T.c, T.\dep, T.\mathit{write\_set})$}
\algline{$T.\lseq \gets$ \textsc{Enqueue}$(\mathit{wal}[s], r)$
         \algcom{append only; no flush, no wait}
         \label{ln:enqueue}}
\algline{$F \gets T.\dep$; \quad $F[s] \gets \max(F[s], T.\lseq)$
         \algcom{closed frontier}
         \label{ln:closed}}
\algline{\textbf{for all} versions $v$ created by $T$ \textbf{do}
         $v.\wfr \gets F$
         \label{ln:pub-w}}
\algline{\textbf{for all} versions $v$ read by $T$ \textbf{do}
         $v.\rfr \gets v.\rfr \fjoin F$
         \algcom{monotone merge}
         \label{ln:pub-r}}
\algline{\textsc{Register}$(F, T)$
         \algcom{durable ack deferred to the committer}
         \label{ln:register}}
\algline{\textbf{return} logical commit
         \algcom{worker proceeds to the next transaction}}
\end{algorithmblock}

\begin{algorithmblock}[t]
\algcaption{Committer-side durable acknowledgment.}
\label{alg:ayame-ack}
\algline{\textbf{State:} durable vector $D$; per-shard waitlists $W[i]$
         ordered by required position}
\algline{\textbf{procedure} \textsc{Register}$(F, T)$
         \algcom{atomic w.r.t.\ \textsc{OnDurableAdvance}}}
\algline{\algind $T.\mathit{remaining} \gets 0$}
\algline{\algind \textbf{for all} shards $i$ such that $F[i] > D[i]$
         \textbf{do}}
\algline{\algind\algind $T.\mathit{remaining} \gets T.\mathit{remaining} + 1$}
\algline{\algind\algind insert $(F[i], T)$ into $W[i]$
         \label{ln:park}}
\algline{\algind \textbf{if} $T.\mathit{remaining} = 0$ \textbf{then}
         \textsc{Ack}$(T)$
         \algcom{own record and all dependencies durable}
         \label{ln:dur-check}}
\algskip
\algline{\textbf{procedure} \textsc{OnDurableAdvance}$(i, d)$
         \algcom{called by flusher $i$ after \texttt{fdatasync}}}
\algline{\algind $D[i] \gets d$}
\algline{\algind \textbf{while} $W[i]$ is not empty and
         \textsc{MinKey}$(W[i]) \le d$ \textbf{do}}
\algline{\algind\algind $(\mathit{need}, T) \gets \textsc{PopMin}(W[i])$}
\algline{\algind\algind $T.\mathit{remaining} \gets T.\mathit{remaining} - 1$}
\algline{\algind\algind \textbf{if} $T.\mathit{remaining} = 0$
         \textbf{then} \textsc{Ack}$(T)$
         \label{ln:ack-ready}}
\end{algorithmblock}

Algorithm~\ref{alg:ayame-dep} accumulates into $T.\dep$ the log
positions that must be durable before $T$ may be acknowledged: reading
a version makes $T$ depend on its writer
(line~\ref{ln:dep-read}), and overwriting a version makes $T$
additionally depend on the readers of the overwritten version
(line~\ref{ln:dep-write}).  Transactions known to be read-only skip
collection entirely.

In Algorithm~\ref{alg:ayame-commit}, the commit timestamp $T.c$ that
ERMIA/SSN assigns for validation doubles as the logical LSN of the log
record (line~\ref{ln:cstamp}); Ayame allocates no WAL-side global
sequence number.  The worker only enqueues the record to its own shard
(line~\ref{ln:enqueue}) and never calls \texttt{fdatasync} or waits
for durability.  The closed frontier $F$ extends $T.\dep$ with $T$'s
own log position (line~\ref{ln:closed}) and is published to the
versions $T$ wrote and read
(lines~\ref{ln:pub-w}--\ref{ln:pub-r}), so that future transactions
inherit it through Algorithm~\ref{alg:ayame-dep}.

Algorithm~\ref{alg:ayame-ack} acknowledges $T$ once $F \le D$, i.e.,
once $T$'s own record and every record $T$ depends on are durable.
A transaction waits only on the shards whose durable position has not
yet reached its requirement (line~\ref{ln:park}); shards unrelated to
$T$ never delay its acknowledgment, unlike a global durable prefix.
Flushers require no algorithm of their own: each flusher owns one WAL
shard, writes the queued records as a single batch, calls
\texttt{fdatasync}, and invokes \textsc{OnDurableAdvance} with the new
durable position.
